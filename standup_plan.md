# Daily Standup Meeting Plan

## Overview
**Duration:** 15 minutes max  
**Frequency:** Daily, same time, same place  
**Format:** Team stands in a circle or uses a video call

---

## Time Breakdown

| Section | Time |
|---|---|
| Opening & Context | 1 min |
| Yesterday | 4 min |
| Today | 6 min |
| Blockers | 3 min |
| Wrap-up | 1 min |
| **Total** | **15 min** |

---

## Team Member Template

Each member shares the following (keep it brief — ~1 minute per person):

- **Yesterday:** What did I complete or work on?
- **Today:** What do I plan to work on?
- **Blockers:** Is anything stopping me from making progress?

**Example:**
> Yesterday: Finished the login API endpoint. Today: Writing unit tests for it. Blockers: Waiting for the QA environment to be ready.

---

## Tips for Keeping Standups Under 15 Minutes

1. **Stand up.** Literally — it keeps people short.
2. **No deep dives.** If a discussion needs more time, take it offline with the relevant people only.
3. **Timebox each person.** Use a visible timer or a gentle reminder app.
4. **Pre-work.** Ask team members to update a shared board (e.g., Kanban) before the meeting.
5. **Skip status reports.** The board already shows progress; focus on what needs help.
6. **Rotate the facilitator.** Keeps energy fresh and shares ownership.
7. **Park side topics.** Use a "parking lot" list for items that don't fit the agenda.
8. **End on time.** Respect the clock — it builds discipline.

---

## Quick Reference for Facilitator

- Start on time, regardless of who's present.
- Go around the room in a consistent order.
- Ask: "What's blocking you?" if someone is vague.
- Close by confirming action items and owners.
- Send a 3-line summary afterward if needed.
