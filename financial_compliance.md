# Financial Services Regulatory Compliance Plan

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## 1. Regulatory Landscape Mapping

Identify and maintain an up-to-date register of all applicable regulations:

| Regulation | Scope | Key Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Basel III** | Capital & liquidity | Minimum capital ratios, LCR, NSFR, leverage ratio |
| **Dodd-Frank** | US systemic risk | Volcker Rule, stress testing, derivatives clearing |
| **MiFID II** | EU markets | Transaction reporting, best execution, product governance |
| **PSD2** | EU payments | Strong customer authentication, open banking APIs |
| **AML/KYC** | Anti-money laundering | Customer due diligence, SAR filing, beneficial ownership |

**Action:** Maintain a living regulatory register reviewed quarterly. Assign a regulation owner for each framework.

---

## 2. Compliance Risk Assessment

Conduct structured risk assessments across three domains:

- **Regulatory Risk:** Changes in law or regulatory guidance; likelihood and impact scoring on a quarterly basis.
- **Operational Risk:** Process failures, third-party dependencies, and technology outages; map to key risk indicators (KRIs).
- **Model Risk:** Validation of quantitative models used in credit, market, and operational risk; independent model validation annually.

**Action:** Produce a consolidated risk heat map each quarter and present to the Compliance Committee.

---

## 3. Governance Framework

### Board Oversight
- The Board approves the compliance strategy and reviews the risk appetite statement annually.
- A dedicated Board Compliance Committee meets quarterly.

### Compliance Committee
- Chaired by the CCO; members include CRO, CIO, General Counsel, and business unit heads.
- Meets monthly to review risk assessments, incidents, and regulatory changes.

### three lines of defense
1. **First Line:** Business units own and manage day-to-day compliance risks.
2. **Second Line:** Compliance and Risk functions set policies, monitor, and challenge.
3. **Third Line:** Internal Audit provides independent assurance to the Board.

**Action:** Define RACI matrices for each compliance process and review annually.

---

## 4. Policy and Procedure Development

- **Policy Lifecycle:** Draft → Review → Approve → Publish → Retire. Policies have a maximum two-year validity.
- **Version Control:** All documents tracked in the central policy repository with change logs.
- **Approval Workflows:**
  - Standard policies: CCO approval.
  - High-risk policies: Board Compliance Committee approval.
  - Emergency updates: CCO may issue interim guidance with Board ratification within 30 days.

**Action:** Maintain a policy register with effective dates, owners, and review schedules.

---

## 5. Monitoring and Reporting

### Regulatory Reporting
- Automated feeds to regulators (e.g., Basel III Pillar 3 disclosures, MiFID II transaction reports).
- Monthly regulatory calendar with submission deadlines tracked in a central tracker.

### Exception Reporting
- Real-time alerts for breach of limits, policy exceptions, and KRI thresholds.
- Escalation to CCO within 24 hours of detection.

### Trend Analysis
- Quarterly analysis of exception trends, audit findings, and regulatory actions.
- Present findings and remediation plans to the Compliance Committee.

---

## 6. Audit Management

| Activity | Frequency | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal Audit** | Annual (risk-based plan) | Full coverage of compliance functions over 3-year cycle |
| **External Audit** | As required | Independent review of financial controls and reporting |
| **Regulatory Examination Readiness** | Ongoing | Mock examinations, document preparedness, staff briefing |

**Action:** Maintain an audit issue tracker with remediation owners and target dates. Close overdue items escalated to the Board.

---

## 7. Training and Awareness

| Program | Audience | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Role-based training** | Sales, operations, risk, compliance | Onboarding + annual refresh |
| **AML/KYC certification** | Customer-facing staff | Annual certification exam |
| **Phishing simulations** | All employees | Quarterly |
| **Board training** | Directors | Annual regulatory update |

**Action:** Track completion rates; non-compliance escalates to HR for performance management.

---

## 8. Technology Enablement

Deploy **regtech** solutions to automate and enhance compliance:

- **Automated Monitoring:** Real-time transaction screening for AML and sanctions.
- **Workflow Automation:** Policy approval routing, issue tracking, and remediation management.
- **Regulatory Change Management:** AI-powered scanning of regulatory feeds with impact classification.
- **Dashboarding:** Unified compliance KRI dashboard for the Board and senior management.

**Action:** Conduct a regtech tool assessment annually and maintain a technology risk assessment for all compliance systems.

---

## 9. Incident Response and Breach Notification

### Regulatory Timelines
| Event Type | Notification Deadline |
|---|---|
| Material compliance breach | 24 hours to CCO, 72 hours to regulator |
| Data breach (GDPR/CCPA) | 72 hours to supervisory authority |
| AML suspicious activity | As prescribed by local FIU (typically within 30 days) |

### Communication Plan
1. **Detect** — Automated alert or manual report.
2. **Contain** — Immediate remediation actions by first-line owner.
3. **Assess** — CCO determines materiality and regulatory obligations.
4. **Notify** — Formal notification to regulators within required timelines.
5. **Report** — Root cause analysis and remediation plan to Board within 14 days.
6. **Learn** — Update policies, controls, and training based on lessons learned.

**Action:** Conduct incident response tabletop exercises biannually.

---

## 10. Cross-Border Compliance

### Jurisdictional Mapping
- Maintain a jurisdictional matrix covering all operating entities.
- Identify local licensing, reporting, and consumer protection requirements per jurisdiction.

### Data Transfer Restrictions
- Map data flows across borders; ensure GDPR, CCPA, and local data localization laws are respected.
- Use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or binding corporate rules (BCRs) for international transfers.
- Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for new cross-border processing.

**Action:** Review the cross-border compliance register annually with Legal and Data Privacy teams.
